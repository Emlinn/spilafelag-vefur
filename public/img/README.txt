Myndir / photos
===============

Drop real photos here with these exact names and they appear automatically.
Until then, the drawn fjord (fjord.svg) and dark gradients stand in.

  hero.jpg            front page, full screen behind the headline   (landscape, ~2400 px wide)
  cta.jpg             the "Vertu með" band near the bottom          (landscape, ~2400 px wide)
  commander.jpg       "Magic fyrst" card 1 — Commander night        (portrait-ish, ~1000 px wide)
  safnkortaspil.jpg   "Magic fyrst" card 2 — Draft / prerelease     (portrait-ish, ~1000 px wide)

Origin's look comes from calm, moody nature photography with the UI floating
on glass over it. Westfjords landscapes (fjords, flat-topped mountains, low
cloud) suit the hero; close, textured shots (felt, card backs, dice, wood,
moss) suit the cards. Keep files under ~400 KB (export JPG at ~75% quality).

Video (optional)
----------------
  hero.mp4            loops behind the headline, over hero.jpg          (H.264, 1920x1080, no sound, ~5-7 MB)

hero.jpg doubles as the video's still: it shows while the video loads, and is all
that visitors with "reduce motion" or data-saver see. Use the first frame of the
clip so the switch is invisible. Never put the raw camera export here — 48 MB
on a phone is far too much.
