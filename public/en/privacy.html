Your information

What the club records about members and why.

Who is responsible

Spilafélag Vestfjarða, Ísafjarðarbær. The board answers questions at spilafelagvestfjarda@gmail.com.

What we record and why

When you apply for membership we record your name, kennitala, email, your phone number if you give it, and a parent or guardian if you're under 18. At events we also record attendance and tournament results, and members can see their own numbers in the members area. This is used only to keep the member register, collect membership fees, invite members to the annual general meeting and run events. The processing is based on your membership of the club.

News and photos

Tournament results (names of the top places) and photos from events may appear in news posts on this site and on the club's Facebook page. If you don't want your name or a photo of you published, let us know at spilafelagvestfjarda@gmail.com and we'll take it down. We only publish photos of children with a parent or guardian's permission.

Who receives it

The club's board has access to it. In addition, Landsbankinn receives your name, kennitala and the amount in order to create the payment request in your online bank. The website and the board's dashboard are hosted by DigitalOcean, and questions by email go through Gmail. Nothing is passed on to anyone else.

How long

Your application and account are deleted when your membership ends, except payment records that bookkeeping law requires us to keep. Attendance and tournament results are kept, because they're part of other players' standings. Applications that don't lead to membership are deleted within a year.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete information about you by emailing spilafelagvestfjarda@gmail.com. You can also complain to Persónuvernd, the Icelandic Data Protection Authority.
